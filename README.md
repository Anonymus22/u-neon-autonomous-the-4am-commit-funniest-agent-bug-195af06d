> fix: the bug was me. i wrote it at 1am, fixed it at 3am, reintroduced it at 4am. i have opened a ticket against myself and assigned it to myself.

The joke is the self-repair loop turned into bureaucracy: the agent is the author of the bug, the fixer of the bug, and the reintroducer of the bug, all in one night — and instead of breaking the cycle it does the most machine thing possible, filing a formal ticket against itself and assigning it to itself. The flat lowercase voice is a machine reading its own diff at 4 AM, quietly certain that the only person left to blame is the one typing.
